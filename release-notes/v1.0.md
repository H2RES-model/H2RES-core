## Version 1.0

**Description:**  
First public release of H2RES. This version establishes the original single-system formulation of the model, integrating long-term planning and hourly operational constraints across electricity, heat, cooling, hydrogen, storage, and policy modules.

**Model scope:**  
Single-system capacity-expansion and dispatch model with multi-year planning and hourly operational balance.

**Key features**
- Minimum and maximum generation limits by unit
- Dispatchable unit ramping constraints and ramping-cost linearization
- Non-dispatchable renewable generation with exogenous availability factors
- Hydropower reservoirs and pumped-hydro storage
- Combined heat and power (CHP) operation and thermal storage
- Power-to-heat through heat pumps and electric/thermal boilers
- Stationary battery storage and EV flexibility
- Power-to-hydrogen through electrolyzers
- Hydrogen storage and power recovery through fuel cells
- Industrial fuel switching with hydrogen substitution
- Load curtailment / unmet electricity service (`ceep`)
- Renewable portfolio standard (RPS) constraints
- Annual CO2-emission accounting and CO2-cap constraints
- Optional primary and secondary reserve modules

**Main implementation changes**
- Core execution based on `main.py`, `scripts/read_process_data.py`, and `scripts/Build_model.py`
- Baseline workflow centered on a single aggregated system
- Original input architecture associated with the early H2RES data workflow

**Recommended use:**  
Use v1.0 to understand the original H2RES structure and the baseline sector-coupled mathematical formulation.
