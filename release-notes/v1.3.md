## Version 1.3

**Description:**  
Fourth public release of H2RES. Version 1.3 extends the model from a single-system rolling-horizon framework to a multizone regional platform with bilateral electricity exchanges, endogenous transmission expansion, and explicit coexistence of regional and national policy constraints.

**Model scope:**  
Multizone rolling-horizon capacity-expansion and dispatch model with region-specific assets, interconnection flows, and aggregated national policy layers.

**Key features**
- Regionalized generation, storage, heat, hydrogen, and industrial-fuel variables
- Explicit multi-system structure through top-level regional sets
- Bilateral electricity-transfer variables between systems
- Optional endogenous transmission-capacity expansion
- Rolling-horizon execution retained as the standard bundled workflow
- Regional renewable portfolio standards and CO2 constraints
- National renewable-share and national CO2-cap policy layers
- Regional EV flexibility, hydrogen production, hydrogen storage, and thermal coupling
- Region-specific and system-aggregate output reporting

**Main implementation changes**
- Active bundled workflow returns to `main.py`
- Execution orchestration remains routed through `scripts/modeling_approaches.py`
- A single multizone builder replaces the earlier separation between standard and rolling-horizon model builders
- Explicit transmission-expansion and bilateral-import structures are added
- Output workflow becomes multizone and rolling-horizon aware
- Data consistency across regional identifiers becomes a central modeling requirement

**Recommended use:**  
Use v1.3 for multiregional planning studies, interconnection analysis, transmission-expansion studies, and policy assessments that combine regional and national constraints in the same optimization framework.
